## Decision

- Per-user history features add little (PR-AUC +0.003, +20 frauds at 1% FPR), most likely
  because the C and D columns already encode card history. Kept for the streaming pipeline.
- Tested normalizing the D columns (day - D): PR-AUC 0.557 → 0.571, +151 frauds at 1% FPR
  (see e2_dnorm.md). Not adopted for now: we keep raw D to stay with the original feature set;
  may revisit after the 3-seed comparison in week 3.
